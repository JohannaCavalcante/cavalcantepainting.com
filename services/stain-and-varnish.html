Stair, Deck & Fence Staining in Toronto

Daily foot traffic indoors and Ontario's hot summers and freezing winters outdoors are tough on wood. Staining and varnishing helps protect your stairs, deck, fence, doors and trim, and brings back their natural warmth and color.

What it is

Wood Staining and Varnishing, Done Right

We stain and varnish outdoor wood like decks, fences, pergolas and railings, and indoor wood like stairs, railings, doors, trim and built-ins. Stain soaks into the wood to add color and protection, while varnish creates a clear, durable top layer.

Before we apply anything, we clean the wood, remove old flaking finish where needed and sand the surface, so the new finish goes on evenly and holds up longer.

Who it's for

Who This Service Is For

  • Homeowners whose deck or fence looks grey, faded or worn

  • New deck or fence owners who want to protect the wood from day one

  • Anyone with wood stairs, railings, doors or trim that have lost their shine

  • Homeowners getting ready for summer, or protecting their wood before winter
