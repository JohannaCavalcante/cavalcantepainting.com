Cabinet & Built-In Spray Painting in Toronto & the GTA

Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, closets, built-ins and wall panels across Toronto and the GTA. We spray-paint cabinets for a smooth, factory-like finish that gives the whole room a fresh, modern feel, without the cost and mess of a full renovation.

What it is

A Fresh New Look Without New Cabinets

Cabinet painting is a full refinishing process for the cabinets in your kitchen, bathroom, laundry room, closets or built-ins: doors, drawer fronts and cabinet boxes. We clean, degrease, sand and prime every surface before applying the finish coats, so the paint bonds well and holds up to daily use.

Where it makes sense, we spray the doors and drawer fronts for a smooth, even finish.

Who it's for

Is Cabinet Painting Right for You?

  • Your cabinets are solid and in good shape, but the color or finish feels dated

  • You want to update your kitchen or bathroom on a smaller budget than a full renovation

  • You're getting your home ready to sell and want the kitchen to stand out

  • You just moved into a home and want to make the kitchen your own
