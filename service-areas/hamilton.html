Painters in Hamilton

Hamilton is full of homes with history, from the brick houses of the lower city to the neighbourhoods on the Mountain, Dundas and Ancaster. Homes like these reward careful work.

Painting in Hamilton

Respecting the Character of Hamilton Homes

Older Hamilton homes often have high baseboards, original doors, wood staircases and walls that have been painted many times over. Good results start with prep: repairing cracks, sanding old layers smooth and priming before the finish coats.

We also help homeowners who are updating an older house step by step. Painting the kitchen cabinets, refinishing the stairs or adding wallpaper to one room can modernize a home while keeping what makes it special.
