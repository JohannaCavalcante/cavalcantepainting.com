FAQ

Painting FAQs for Toronto & GTA Homeowners

Here are answers to the questions we hear most often. Don't see yours? Send us a message on WhatsApp and we'll be happy to help.

Estimates & Pricing

Yes. Every estimate is free and comes with no obligation. We visit your home or business anywhere in our service area, look at the job in person and talk about what you have in mind. Then we send you a clear, detailed price.

The price depends on the size of the space, the number of rooms or surfaces, the condition of the walls and the finish you choose. A single room and a full exterior are very different projects. That's why we don't give prices over the phone: we evaluate everything on-site so your estimate is accurate. There are no surprises once the work starts.

Photos help, but they don't show everything. In person, we can check for cracks, peeling, moisture or old finishes that need extra prep. This lets us give you a price that reflects the real job.

Your estimate explains the work to be done, the surfaces included, the prep needed and the timeline. If you have questions about any part of it, we'll walk you through it.

Payment

We'll explain the payment terms clearly in your estimate, before any work starts.

Any deposit and the payment schedule are written in your estimate, so you know exactly what to expect.

Scheduling & Timelines

It depends on the size and type of work. A single room usually takes much less time than a full interior, an exterior or a set of kitchen cabinets. You get a realistic timeline with your estimate, so you can plan around it. We keep you updated if anything changes, like weather for outdoor work.

It depends on our schedule at the time you contact us. Spring and summer are our busiest seasons, so we recommend reaching out early for exterior and deck projects. Send us a message and we'll tell you our next available dates.

Exterior painting is usually done from late spring to early fall, when temperatures are mild and the weather is drier. Paint needs the right conditions to dry and bond properly. We plan outdoor work around the forecast to protect the finish. Interior painting can be done all year round.
