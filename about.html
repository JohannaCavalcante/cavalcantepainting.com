About us

About Cavalcante Painting Company, Painters in Toronto

Our story

A Family Painting Business in Toronto

Cavalcante Painting Company is a family-owned painting company serving Toronto and the GTA. With more than 15 years of painting experience behind us, we believe a great paint job is all in the details: careful prep, clean lines and a finish that lasts.

From interior and exterior spray painting to cabinet painting, stair staining and wallpaper installation, we treat every home as if it were our own. We protect your floors and furniture, keep the work area tidy, and leave every space clean when the job is done.

Every project starts with a free estimate and honest advice, so you know exactly what to expect. Our goal is simple: a beautiful result and a client who calls us again.

Every detail, beautifully done.
